  ## Assessment A: Design Review, homepage of marche.be (blog CITOYEN)

  ### 1) Design specificity verdict: fails, and it is the named anti-reference

  Another commune could use this page unchanged. It is close to a line-by-line

  - **Icon tiles:** a 3×2 grid of rounded-xl tiles with `hover:-translate-y-1

  rounded-full icon circles with `scale-110` (`_widgets.html.twig:10-12`).

  editors' poster images. There is no photograph of the place. The dark

  - **Labels:** month labels are `uppercase`.

  (`_news.html.twig:3`, `_agenda.html.twig:3`). This is the side-stripe pattern

  ### 3) Cognitive load

  Checklist failures: 5 of 8, which the rubric rates high.
  - **Single focus:** fails. The hero has search, then 6 tiles straight away,
  then two content columns.
  - **Chunking:** fails. Two groups of 6 tiles, with no meaning to the split
  between them. "Emploi" appears in both groups.
  - **Visual hierarchy:** fails. The H1 greeting is the biggest thing on screen
  and carries no information.
  - **Minimal choices:** fails.
  - **Progressive disclosure:** fails. Partners and webmail sit at the same
  level as civic tasks.
  - Working memory and grouping pass. One-thing-at-a-time is borderline.

  Decision points with more than 4 visible options:
  - **Desktop header:** 6 nav items, plus search and 3 social icons, so 10
  targets.
  - **Shortcuts:** 6 items.
  - **Widgets:** 6 items.
  - **Partners:** 7 logos.
  - **Mobile above the fold:** the header has 5 targets (hamburger, search, 3

  ### 4) Emotional journey

  1. **Arrival:** a generic welcome sign. It is polite but anonymous, with a
  cool slate wash and no sense of place.
  2. **Search:** competent and reassuring.
  3. **Tiles:** "is my task here?" The resident scans 6 labels, doesn't find
  "Collectes" or "Conseil communal", and starts to doubt.
  4. **News:** livens up thanks to the editors' posters (Atelier Nature, Noël à

  6. **Widgets:** a second tile wall that feels like repetition.
  7. **"Nos Partenaires":** marketing tone ("dynamiser notre commune").
  8. **Footer:** a dark slab. The page ends on corporate rather than civic.

● Homepage brief: marche.be (CITOYEN)

  1. Job and audience
  - Who: a resident of Marche, often older, often on a phone, who wants a civic
    answer in under a minute: collection day, the next council meeting, an
    online procedure, opening hours.
  - Mode: Operate/Read. The page is an index to answers, not a welcome mat.

  2. Outcome and proof
  - Success: Monique finds her collection calendar or the next council date from
    the first screen, without searching.
  - Proof of being of Marche: a real photo of the town centre
    (assets/images/bh_home2.jpg), and the next council meeting as a fact on the
    page.

  3. Direction
  - Visual system: the established "Famenne Almanac" in DESIGN.md. No new visual
    world.
  - Thesis: the page reads like an almanac index. Facts and plain-word tasks
    come first, the place shows in one photograph, and the page has no icons,
    tiles, gradients or hover-lift.
  - Focal point: the search field (the page's one ember button) plus the line
    "Prochain conseil communal".

  4. Order of the page

  1. Hero
     - Left column:
       - An H1 at headline size, "Ville de Marche-en-Famenne", not the 60px
         "Bienvenue".
       - The search field.
       - The fact line: "Prochain conseil communal : lundi 7 décembre, 19h ·

       above the fold.

  3. Six main tasks: a list of rows separated by hairline rules, not cards. Each
     row is a text title (title style) with a one-line description (ink-soft),
     and the whole row is the link. It sits in 3 columns on desktop, 2 on tablet
     and 1 on mobile.

     b. Conseil communal (#1650)

     d. Horaires et contact
     e. Travaux en cours
     f. Avis de publication
  4. News and agenda: each column keeps its natural height, and the agenda gets

  5. Autres services: one compact list of text links: CPAS, Avis de décès,

  - Next council date:
    - Rule: first Monday of the month at 19h, no meeting in August.

  - Reduced motion: honoured.
